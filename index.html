About Us

The International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON) is a worldwide community of devotees practicing bhakti-yoga, the science of devotional service to Lord Krishna. Our new center in Datia aims to provide a sanctuary for spiritual seekers, offering programs, classes, and community events to foster spiritual growth and understanding of Vedic wisdom.

Founded on the principles of compassion, service, and devotion, ISKCON Datia welcomes all who wish to explore the path of bhakti and experience the joy of Krishna consciousness.
